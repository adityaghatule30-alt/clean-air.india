# 🌤️ CleanAir India

### Weather & Air Quality — but make it fun. 😂

CleanAir India is an interactive weather and air-quality web app that combines
real environmental data with playful city banter, comparisons, and mini-games.

Because checking the weather shouldn't feel like reading a spreadsheet. 😭

---

## 🌐 Live Demo

### 👉 [**Open CleanAir India →**](https://clean-air-india.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ What Can You Do?

### 🌡️ Check the Weather
Get useful weather information for your location:

- Current temperature
- Feels-like temperature
- Weather conditions
- Humidity
- Wind
- Precipitation
- Forecast information

### 🫁 Check the Air

See current air-quality information including:

- AQI
- PM2.5
- PM10
- Air-quality category
- Data freshness

No fake numbers. If reliable data isn't available, the app tells you.

### 📍 Explore Locations

Search for different cities and locations and instantly explore their
weather and air quality.

## 💬 City Group Chat

Ever wondered what cities would say about each other's weather?

Now you don't have to.

Cities can react to real weather and air-quality conditions with
context-aware fictional conversations.

Example:

> **Jaipur:** 41°C. I'm officially the hottest. 🔥  
>
> **Shimla:** 15°C btw. 😌  
>
> **Delhi:** AQI 190. I have bigger problems. 💀  
>
> **Pune:** AQI 26. 🌿  
>
> **Delhi:** Nobody likes a show-off.

The conversation is fictional.

The underlying weather and air-quality data is real.

## 🔄 Data & Updates

CleanAir India uses external weather and air-quality data sources.

Data availability and update frequency depend on the underlying APIs.

The application therefore uses wording such as:

**"Latest available data"**

rather than claiming that every value is updated every second.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The idea is simple:

> **Make useful environmental information easier — and more fun — to explore.**

Instead of opening a boring weather dashboard, you can:

**Check the weather → check the air → compare cities → play a game → laugh at your city's situation.**

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

CleanAir India is an informational and entertainment project.

Weather and air-quality information may have delays or limitations depending
on the underlying data providers.

For health, safety, severe-weather warnings, or emergency decisions, always
refer to appropriate official sources.
